SHIPPING & RETURNS

Last Updated: April 4, 2026

We work hard to get your home gym kit to you as fast as possible. Below is everything you need to know about our shipping and return processes.

1. SHIPPING TIMES

Orders are typically processed within 24–48 hours. Once shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email.

  • USA: 3–5 Business Days (Express) / 7–12 Days (Standard)
  • International: 10–20 Business Days

2. SHIPPING COSTS

We offer FREE EXPRESS SHIPPING on all orders over $50. For orders under $50, a flat rate of $9.95 applies.

3. 60-DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE

We stand behind the quality of our equipment. If you are not 100% satisfied with your results or the equipment itself, you can return it within 60 days of purchase for a full refund.

To start a return, simply email us at info@healthhustleacademy.com with your order number.

4. DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS

If your order arrives damaged or with any defects, please notify us immediately. We will ship out a replacement at no cost to you within 24 hours of confirmation.

5. CANCELLATIONS

Orders can be cancelled within 12 hours of purchase. Once an order has been processed and a tracking number issued, cancellations are no longer possible, and the standard return process must be followed.

6. CONTACT US

Questions about your shipment? Email us anytime at info@healthhustleacademy.com.
