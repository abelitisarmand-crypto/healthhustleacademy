ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

1. Our Commitment

HealthHustle Academy is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards to our storefront.

2. Standards We Follow

We aim to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, ensuring a better experience for everyone regardless of how they navigate the web.

3. Known Limitations

  • We are actively working to fix all issues identified in our April 2026 accessibility audit.
  • Some third-party content (such as Shopify-hosted scripts) may not yet meet full accessibility standards.

4. Contact Us

If you experience any barriers to accessibility on our website, please let us know. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2 business days.
