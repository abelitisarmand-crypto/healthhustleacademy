HOW TO ADD PRODUCTS

Follow these steps to ensure your new products appear correctly and are optimized for conversion on the HealthHustleAcademy Storefront.

01
CREATE PRODUCT

Go to Shopify Admin → Products → Add Product. Fill in your title, description, and high-quality lifestyle imagery.

02
ACTIVATE STATUS

In the top right corner, set the Product Status to "Active". Draft products will not be visible on the site.

03
SELECT PUBLISHING

Under Sales Channels, ensure the "Health Hustle Academy Headless" channel is checked. This allows the API to fetch the data.

04
ASSIGN COLLECTIONS

Add the product to at least one category collection (e.g., Lose Weight, Build Strength, Move More, or Recover Faster).

05
FEATURE ON HOMEPAGE

To show the product in the default "BEST SELLERS" tab on the homepage, add it to the collection with the handle "Featured".

06
MANAGE INVENTORY

Set an inventory count (e.g., 50) or check "Continue selling when out of stock". Products with 0 stock and no backorder will be hidden or show 'Sold Out'.

07
SAVE & SYNC

Click Save. Your product will appear on the storefront within 60 seconds. Refresh your browser to see the changes.
